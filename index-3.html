About

I am a public health scientist and epidemiologist working at the intersection of health systems, implementation research and evidence-informed policy. My work focuses on translating research into practical approaches that improve access, quality and equity in health services.

Across more than a decade of work in East and Southern Africa and Europe, I have contributed to research, programme leadership and technical collaboration spanning women’s health, sexual and reproductive health and rights, HIV and tuberculosis, non-communicable diseases, gender-based violence and health-system strengthening.

I hold a PhD in Public Health and Epidemiology from Heidelberg University. My approach combines epidemiological and mixed-methods research, programme design, partnership development and the use of data to support decision-making.
